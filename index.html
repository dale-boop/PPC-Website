Paper Plane Creative is a brand video and photography studio based in Durban. We work with brands across South Africa to produce content that actually earns attention - not just ticks a box.

We're not chasing virality. We're building the visual stories that make people stop, feel something, and remember who you are. Every project starts with one question: what does this brand need to say, and what's the most honest way to say it?

When you work with us, you're not briefing a vendor. You're getting a creative department - really invested in making the work land.
