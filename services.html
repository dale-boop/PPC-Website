Service 03

Audio
Studio

"Audio built for the story."

Most productions end up with a royalty-free track and call it done. We don't work that way. For every major project, we write and produce the audio ourselves - scored from the brief, built around the emotion the piece needs to land. A brand film that genuinely moves people sounds different from one that just has background music. That difference starts with treating the audio as seriously as the visuals.

We also run a full recording studio out of Durban - so if you need podcast production, voice recording, or sound design for any other purpose, that's all here too.

What's included

  • Custom scoring & original composition
  • Brand film audio production
  • Sound design
  • Studio recording sessions
  • Podcast & video podcast production
  • Audio editing, mixing & mastering

How We
Work

Every project moves through four stages. No surprises, no chasing. Just a clean process that keeps everyone moving in the same direction.

01

Brief

We get inside your brand - the goals, the audience, the feeling you're after. This is where the creative direction takes shape.

02

Develop

Script, shot list, mood board, locations - and where the project calls for it, an audio brief too. Every detail is mapped before a single camera is unpacked.

03

Produce

Shoot day. We run a tight set - focused, efficient, and always chasing the best light in the room.

04

Deliver

Edit, grade, mix, and hand off. You get distribution-ready files and formats sized for every platform you need.
