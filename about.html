Who We Are

Two people.
One
department.

Paper Plane Creative is a brand video and photography studio based in Durban. We started in September 2023 with a simple belief - that brands doing great work deserve content that actually does that work justice. Not just polished. Not just on-trend. Honest, well-crafted storytelling that connects.

Between us, we cover every part of a production - strategy, shooting, editing, and delivery. You always know who you're talking to, and nothing gets lost in a chain of freelancers and account managers. We're really hands-on like that, and our clients love it.

We're small by design. We work with a select number of clients at any one time, so every project gets the focus it deserves.

Our Approach

Story
first.
Always.

We don't arrive on set with a shot list and a deadline. We arrive with questions. What is this brand really trying to say? Who are they saying it to? What do we want those people to feel when it's over? The answers shape everything - the framing, the edit, the music, the pacing.

Good content gets watched. Great content gets remembered. The difference almost always comes down to story. We obsess over that - not just because it's good craft, but because it's what actually moves the needle for the brands we work with.

We're also really practical. Deadlines matter, briefs change, and budgets are real. We know how to move fast without cutting corners on the things that count.

"When you work with us, you're not briefing a vendor. You're getting a creative department - with a genuine stake in the outcome."

How We Work

01

Story First

Every brief starts with the story, not the deliverable. We don't open a project doc until we understand what the brand is trying to say and why it matters. The video or photo is the output - the story is the real work.

02

Intentional, Not Busy

We don't make content to fill a calendar. Every piece we produce should earn its place - online, in a pitch deck, on a screen. If we can't tell you exactly why something should exist, we won't make it. Simple as that.

03

Built for the Long Game

We measure success in brand equity, not view counts. A film that genuinely connects with 500 of the right people is worth more than one that reaches 50,000 who scroll straight past. We work with clients who think the same way.

04

Partners, Not Vendors

When you work with Paper Plane Creative, you get two people with a genuine stake in your outcome. We push back when we should, bring ideas unprompted, and really care about how the work performs long after the files are delivered.
